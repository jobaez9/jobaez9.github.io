A data application for comparing players, teams, and scouts side by side.

Overview

This app allows users to compare soccer players, teams, and scouts against one another, surfacing side-by-side statistics to support recruitment decisions and performance analysis. It is currently running locally while in development, with a public demo planned for the future.
